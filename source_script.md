need to fix the defense. Three of our losses came down to one goal. But that's not
what the numbers actually say. In those close losses, we didn't give up more goals

taking any shots. If we get her the ball more, we're probably looking at somewhere
around two-thirds of a goal a game, and that's exactly the margin we lost those close
games by. So the plan is pretty simple: get Vogelman shooting more.
